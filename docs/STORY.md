# Parry Bros: story and campaign plan

This is the creator's plan for the whole game. Read it before working on story,
bosses, cutscenes, chapters, lobbies, unlocks or rewards. When the creator
decides something new, update this file in the same change.

Each item is marked:

- **Decided**: the creator has confirmed it.
- **Leaning**: the creator likes it, but it isn't locked.
- **Open**: not decided yet. Ask, don't invent.

Plans have changed over time. If anything here conflicts with what the
creator says now, what they say now wins; then fix this file.

---

## Campaign structure

- **Decided:** Launch with Chapters 1 and 2, possibly Chapter 3. Chapters 4 and 5
  may follow if the game does well.
- **Decided:** Each chapter has its own 50-floor progression and its own lobby.
  Later chapters probably won't be a literal climb, since a tower doesn't make
  sense for most of them.
- **Decided:** Unique bosses on floors 10, 20, 25, 30, 40 and 50. Random-pool
  bosses on floors 5, 15, 35 and 45.
- **Decided:** Each chapter has a pool of at least 6 random bosses.
  - **Open:** whether each chapter has its own pool or they're shared.
- **Decided:** Finishing a chapter unlocks new things, to keep the game fresh.
  - **Leaning:** the Super Bros could be one chapter's reward.
  - **Open:** which chapter, and what the other chapters unlock.
- **Lobbies (concepts):** Tower camp (Chapter 1), infernal refuge (2), travelling
  headquarters (3), forest sanctuary (4), celestial gateway (5).

## The opening

- **Decided:** The masked Orc King and his silent lieutenant slaughter your
  family and kidnap your brother. Your character trains his parry in a
  montage, then arrives at the ominous Tower.
- **Decided:** The opening is a separate cutscene, not played in the game
  arena. It takes place somewhere other than the Tower.
  - **Open:** where, the art style, and how long it runs.
- **Decided:** The masked lieutenant is **not** your brother. That twist was removed.
- **Open:** whether the existing training mode is the "training montage" or a
  separate scene.

## Chapter 1: The Tower

You climb the Tower pursuing your brother.

| Floor | Boss | Concept |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Iron Warden | Large evil knight with hammer slams and heavy shockwaves. |
| 20 | Illusionist | Illusions, surrounding clones, fake attacks, darkness, a frantic finale. |
| 25 | Masked Wraith (the lieutenant) | The King's silent right-hand man. A blitzing fighter. His mask cracks as he weakens and falls off when he's beaten; the mask becomes armour. He **survives** and returns on floor 50. He never speaks. |
| 30 | Pyromaniac | Fire cult leader. Halfway through, you protect your bros from arena-wide fire with timed taps. |
| 40 | Necromancer | Raises three weakened earlier bosses before you fight him directly. |
| 50 | Orc King | Three phases with interactive cutscenes. See below. |
| 5, 15, 35, 45 | Random pool | **Open.** Needs at least 6 bosses. |

### The Orc King (floor 50)

- **Decided: his weapon is a hammer.** The hand that's cut off at the end of phase 2 is
  the hammer hand. The severed hand and the hammer land at the side of the arena and
  stay there through phase 3. In the ending he picks the hammer back up and uses it
  for the blow that knocks you off the Tower.
- **Decided: phase changes.** Phase 1 runs from full health to 70%, phase 2 from 70%
  to 35%, and phase 3 from 35% to the end.

**Phase 1: Masked.** (Decided)
- He wears his cult mask and fights as a fairly basic boss.
- A short cutscene ends the phase: his mask falls off, he gets angry, and his
  special eyes are revealed.

**Phase 2: The eyes.** (Decided)
- Each eye has a different power:
  - **The sight eye.** This is the eye you take at the end of the chapter, and
    it's where the One-Eyed class gets its powers: seeing through illusions,
    early warnings of attacks, and Cursed Gaze (freezing). In this phase he
    uses those powers *against* you, for example freezing your bros or
    mixing fake attacks in with real ones.
  - **The command eye.** He keeps this one. It summons and empowers his cult's
    enemies. That explains the enemies in phase 3, why he leads the cult, and
    it carries into Chapter 3.
- **Open:** the exact moves for each eye.
- **Decided:** The phase ends with an interactive cutscene:
  1. His hand crushes you.
  2. Your heart splits, and you mash to pull it back together (the
     Undertale-inspired heart idea lives here now).
  3. You unleash a flurry of cuts, and his hand is minced and cut off.

**Phase 3: Enraged.** (Decided)
- He throws off his cloak and attacks fast and relentlessly, mixing in bombs,
  fire and enemies.
- **Decided:** With his weapon arm gone, he falls back on everything his cult
  taught Chapter 1's bosses: the Warden's shockwaves, the Illusionist's fakes,
  the Wraith's dashes, the Pyromaniac's fire and bombs, and the Necromancer's
  summons. The finale tests everything the player learned this chapter.
- **Open:** the exact combos and their order.

**The ending.**
- **Decided:** A timed-tap sequence where you take out his sight eye.
- **Decided:** The lieutenant comes back, and you kill him for good.
- **Decided:** That distracts you, so the King retaliates and sends you flying
  through the Tower wall with his hammer, grabbed from where it fell at the side
  of the arena. You fall into Hell. The King survives.
- **Open:** the exact order. One reading is: eye → lieutenant attacks → you
  kill him → the King strikes. Confirm before building it.
- **Decided:** The win has to feel rewarding before the fall.
  - **Leaning:** the victory/reward screen starts to appear, the King
    interrupts, the screen shatters, and the fall-into-Hell cutscene plays.
  - **Open:** when the player actually receives the rewards: after the fall,
    at the start of Chapter 2, or on a screen after the cutscene.

**Still open for the King:**
- His finished look.
- His health (the phase-change points are decided: 70% and 35%).
- Every individual move, including phase 1's hammer attacks.
- The rewards.
- The timed-tap tuning: how many taps, how wide the window is, how much he
  heals on a miss, and whether Easy or Nightmare change it.

### The One-Eyed class

- **Decided:** It unlocks by beating Chapter 1 on Hard. The eye is the King's
  sight eye.
- **Already in the game:**
  - It sees through illusions.
  - Parry timing is 10% more forgiving.
  - At level 5 it gets early attack warnings.
  - Cursed Gaze freezes all enemies for 4 seconds, and they take triple damage.

## Chapter 2: Hell

- **Decided:** You fight through Hell. The Devil is the final boss.
  - **Open:** the other bosses and their moves.
- **Decided, keep word for word.** The Devil's final words:
  > "You think his servants were born in your age? The cult has broken open
  > time itself. Every age has its monsters… and he is gathering them."
- **Decided:** Then briefly show a revolver, a katana, a chariot wheel, and the
  King's unfinished mechanical arm.
- **Decided:** After escaping Hell, a short montage shows you back at the Tower,
  wiping out its defenders because you're stronger now. A little character at
  the top says the King has already left with your brother.
- **Open:** the conditions during the Devil fight that unlock the secret
  endings (see Chapter 5).

## Chapter 3: Hunting the cult

- **Decided:** You hunt cult leaders from different eras while trying to get your
  brother back. It's an Assassin's Creed-style hunt.
- **Concepts:** a samurai, a Wild West boss, a medieval boss, a Spartan with a
  chariot, and a pirate who fires cannons to pressure you.
- **Decided:** The floor-50 boss is the returning Orc King. He has a bionic arm
  and lots of technology, and each phase brings in new tech. He still has his
  command eye.
- **Open:**
  - His equipment.
  - The brother's rescue scene.
  - The King's final fate.

## Chapter 4: The enchanted forest

- **Decided:** A magical forest realm, ending with the Forest Queen.
- **Decided:** You beat her down to a weakened form and she begs for mercy.
  You choose **Spare** or **Kill**:
  - The first time you pick Kill, your brother steps in and reasons with you.
  - Picking Kill a second time actually kills her.
  - The choice changes your brother's opinion of you and affects the ending.
- **Leaning:** a dragon protection scene, possibly here. The dragon is dying,
  you're hurt and defenceless, and your bros step in front of you to protect
  you. It mirrors the Pyromaniac scene: there you protected them, here they
  protect you.

## Chapter 5: God

You confront God because of all the killing you've done.

**Endings (concepts):**
1. **True ending:** Spare the Queen, keep your brother's respect, meet extra
   conditions, and defeat God.
2. **Mourned death:** Spare the Queen, but God defeats you. Your brother mourns you.
3. **Betrayal:** Kill the Queen. Your brother betrays you just as you're about
   to kill God.
4. **Secret, refuse:** This needs certain conditions from the Devil encounter.
   After your brother betrays you and leaves you bleeding out, the Devil
   offers a deal. You refuse, die, and suffer eternal punishment in Hell.
5. **Secret, accept:** You accept the deal, embrace evil, kill God with the
   Devil's power, and kill your brother in a rage.

- **Open:** the exact requirements for every ending.

## Systems that span the whole game

- **Decided: interactive cutscenes** inspired by God of War, using classic
  timed taps or clicks.
  - Missing a tap makes the boss heal a little and combat resumes.
  - Lower his health again and you get another attempt.
  - The Pyromaniac's protect-your-bros scene already works like this: 3 taps,
    a miss heals him 8%, and you retry at half health. Reuse that system.
- **Decided:** Cutscenes are skippable once you've seen them.
- **Decided: practice arena.** Bosses you've met appear there, including the
  King, but each phase stays locked until you've reached it in a real fight.
- **Decided:** Optional NPC dialogue for players who enjoy it.
- **Boss taunts after repeated deaths** (like "Back again?"):
  - **Leaning:** only some bosses taunt, and the lieutenant always stays silent.
  - It might be cut because of a lore problem: it raises the question of why
    the hero keeps reviving and his injuries heal.
  - **Open:** a lore reason for the restart loop. One idea, *not* confirmed:
    the cult has "broken open time", which could explain the loop and let
    bosses remember you.

## What's built so far

Code facts, so plans and code stay in sync:

- **Built:**
  - The first five unique Tower bosses: Iron Warden, Illusionist, Masked Wraith,
    Pyromaniac and Necromancer.
  - The floor-25 mask-drop scene.
  - The Pyromaniac's protect-your-bros scene.
  - **Chapter 1 ends at floor 50.** Clearing it plays a placeholder "Chapter 1 /
    Complete" cutscene and a results screen. Clearing it on Hard or Nightmare
    unlocks the One-Eyed. Progress is kept in `meta.chapters`. Until the Orc King
    exists, floor 50 uses a random-pool boss.
  - **A shared system for interactive moments** (`QTE` in `index.html`): timed
    taps and mashing, with the heal-a-little-and-retry rule (`qteHealBack`). The
    Pyromaniac's scene runs on it.
  - **A cutscene player** (`CUTSCENES`): shots drawn on the game canvas, tap to
    move on, and a Skip button only for cutscenes already seen.
  - **A heart-split effect already exists** (`startHeart`, used by Last Stand
    and on death). It's a starting point for the phase 2 heart scene.
- **Planned, not built:**
  - The opening cutscene.
  - The Orc King and his cutscenes.
  - The shattering reward screen and the fall into Hell.
  - Chapters 2–5.
- **The random-pool bosses in the game today:** the Bell Tyrant, the Hollow
  Choir, the Bell Ringer and the Three Sisters. That's 4; the plan needs at
  least 6.
- **The Masked Wraith appears on floor 25 only.** He's meant to return in the
  King's ending, which isn't built yet.
- **The Super Bros already exist** (the Warden, Samurai, Wick the Candle Knight,
  Joe & Moe, Phoenix and the Bro King). They're unlocked through the Bro
  Portal, not as a chapter reward. That would need to change if they become a
  chapter's reward.
