Parry

Synergy

Hold the center with one sword. Time every hit. Climb the keep.

Tap anywhere just before an attack lands. The ring closing on each attack turns gold once you can parry it, then white for the last split second. That's a Perfect, and it fires the shot back.
Move with the pad in the bottom-left corner. Attacks follow you, so footwork buys time and picks up gold.
Don't spam. Swinging at nothing drops your sword for a moment. When you fall, you keep your Embers for the Sanctum.
